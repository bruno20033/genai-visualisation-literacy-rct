\section{Deviations from the Pre-Registered Analysis Plan}
\label{app:deviations}

This trial was pre-registered on the Open Science Framework (registration
\texttt{yw3nm}, registered 15 July 2026, under embargo until 10 August 2026).
The registration comprises the pre-registration form, a frozen extract of the
Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP), and the analysis code. That frozen SAP extract
is reproduced, materially unchanged, as Section~\ref{sec:sap} and
Appendix~\ref{app:sap-extended} of this thesis. Recruitment opened on 16 July
2026; every change recorded below therefore post-dates data collection and is
reported as a transparent post-commencement amendment rather than a
finalisation of the plan.

The governing principle is unchanged by any item below: the confirmatory record
(Table~\ref{tab:verdicts}) rests solely on the pre-registered tests, executed as
registered; analyses added after commencement are reported as exploratory and
carry no error-rate claim; and each departure is stated here with its rationale
and its bearing on the conclusions. None of the deviations alters a confirmatory
verdict.

\subsection{Design and sampling (pre-registration form)}
\label{app:dev-design}

\paragraph{Sample size.} The registration fixed a target of $N=200-250$ (66 per arm),
recruited on a rolling basis until reached. Recruitment continued slightly past 
the minimal target of 200 as after the first 30 participants completed the survey faster freeing up budget to extend the study.  Table~\ref{tab:dev-n}. 
The over-enrolment is in the conservative
direction (it raises power for the primary immediate-wave contrast and does not
affect Type~I error) and the allocation ratio was preserved.

\paragraph{Placeholders resolved after registration.} Two values left open at
registration were fixed as data collection began, consistent with the plan's own
instructions: the GenAI-usage covariate was mapped to its survey item at
fielding, and the direct-adoption latency cut-off was set to its pre-specified
default of 5\,s. The plan had anticipated locking this cut-off from a pilot
$\Delta$ distribution; as no pilot was run (a pre-registered design choice), the
default was used and the primary latency read remains, as planned, threshold-free
(the per-arm $\Delta$ distribution and the log-$\Delta$ contrast).

\subsection{Statistical Analysis Plan}
\label{app:dev-sap}

The SAP as published in this thesis (Section~\ref{sec:sap};
Appendix~\ref{app:sap-extended}) is materially identical to the registered SAP:
every model, contrast, estimand anchoring, multiplicity rule, and robustness
item is carried over unchanged. One prose-level change was made after
registration --- the answer-adoption-latency paragraph
(Section~\ref{sec:sap}) was expanded with additional interpretive caveats
treating response latency as a suggestive rather than definitive index of
processing. This is an interpretation-only elaboration: the planned analysis
(threshold-free per-arm $\Delta$ distribution plus a log-$\Delta$ mixed-model
contrast and a direct-adoption rate) is unchanged.

\subsection{Analysis code}
\label{app:dev-code}

\paragraph{Conforms to the plan.} The confirmatory and key-secondary pipeline
was executed as pre-registered: the primary accuracy mixed model with the wave
dummy anchored at the immediate assessment and the covariate adjustment; the
fixed-sequence gatekeeper (H1$_a$ superiority $\rightarrow$ H2$_a$
non-inferiority via the 90\% interval against the 8.5\,pp margin $\rightarrow$
the supporting contrast, stopping at the first non-rejection); the wave-stratified
Cox model with arm$\times$wave terms and participant-clustered (Lin--Wei
sandwich) covariance for its contrasts; the persistence simple effect
$\beta_2+\beta_5$; the Bloom item-level GEE with its Bayesian corroboration; the
productivity models under false-discovery control; multiple imputation
($m=50$) and Lee bounds for attrition; and the robustness battery of
Appendix~\ref{app:robustness}. The covariates are the two pre-registered
baseline measures, and no post-randomisation exclusion enters the primary
analysis. The departures are the following.

\paragraph{Departures from the registered analysis.} Table~\ref{tab:dev-code}
lists each departure, what was registered, what was executed, and the bearing on
the conclusions.

    changes a confirmatory verdict.}

    \toprule

    \midrule
    Per-protocol set (non-inferiority co-analysis)
      & Exclude participants who \emph{disclosed} external LLM / AI-overview use
        during training.
      & An \emph{engagement} (received-treatment) filter --- participants with
        $\geq 1$ interaction with the assigned tool ($n=203$). Non-inferiority
        is declared under both the intention-to-treat and this per-protocol set,
        and a disclosure-based exclusion is additionally reported as a robustness
        check; the two should be reconciled so the pre-registered
        disclosure-based set formally carries the co-analysis. \\
    Randomisation-inference resolution
      & 10{,}000 permutations of the arm labels.
      & 2{,}000 permutations. A supporting robustness check only; the reported
        permutation $p$ is stable at this resolution. Recommended to be raised to
        10{,}000 for the final report. \\
    Cognitive-load ordered-trend test
      & Jonckheere--Terpstra test against the pre-registered arm ordering on the
        load subscales.
      & Not implemented; each subscale was compared across arms with the
        covariate-adjusted arm-contrast regressions instead. A manipulation
        check informing interpretation only. \\
    Socratic-fidelity reliability
      & Human double-coding of a sample of assistant turns with Cohen's $\kappa
        \geq 0.80$.
      & Not implemented as a human $\kappa$; fidelity was quantified by an
        automated per-turn judge score (mean 3.55/5; 6.7\% of turns below the
        pass threshold; no regeneration required) together with a pre-deployment
        adversarial leak audit (Appendix~\ref{app:socratic-validation}). A
        manipulation check; it still evidences that the scaffold withheld
        answers. \\
    Unstructured-covariance sensitivity
      & An unstructured two-wave covariance fitted and reported in place of the
        compound-symmetric model if a likelihood-ratio test rejected the
        equal-variance restriction.
      & The likelihood-ratio comparison was not run; the random-intercept
        (compound-symmetric) model was used throughout. The immediate-wave
        primary contrasts are point-identified independently of the cross-wave
        covariance, so this bears only on the precision of the persistence
        estimand. \\
    Lower-order sparing equivalence test
      & An equivalence (TOST) test of the lower-order Unrestricted--control
        difference against a 6.25\,pp bound, should lower-order sparing be
        claimed.
      & Not implemented. It was moot: the Bloom interaction reversed sign
        (the deficit fell in the \emph{lower}-order items), so no lower-order
        sparing was available to test (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq2}). \\
    Complier average causal effect
      & Two-stage least squares with assignment instrumenting compliant tool use.
      & Reported as the equivalent one-sided-noncompliance Wald ratio (a point
        estimate without a standard error). Because compliance was near-total
        ($\approx 90$--$97\%$ by arm), the complier effect coincides with the
        intention-to-treat effect and the missing interval is immaterial. \\
    Item-level missingness
      & Unanswered items scored incorrect (accuracy) / censored (success time).
      & Applied as registered for items that were administered but left blank;
        items from a block that was never administered are dropped as missing
        rather than scored incorrect. Affects per-participant denominators only
        at the margins. \\

\paragraph{Additional analyses not in the pre-registration.} Three analyses were
added after commencement and are reported as exploratory, without confirmatory
claims, in the Results chapter (Section~\ref{sec:results}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the arm difference in \emph{abstention} (``I do not know'') on the
        unaided post-test, modelled with a participant-clustered logistic
        regression (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq3});
  \item a content-based classification of the unrestricted arm's practice-block
        interactions into cognitive-outsourcing categories, with its coding
        protocol and reliability documented in
        Appendix~\ref{app:outsourcing-coding};
  \item a within-arm outsourcing dose--response relating the amount of
        outsourced practice to unaided post-test accuracy, reported as
        associational only.
\end{enumerate}
The pre-registered arm-by-Mini-VLAT moderation was itself specified as
exploratory and is likewise reported without a verdict; it is not a new
addition.

\paragraph{Pre-registered analysis not implemented.} The plan named an
erroneous-answer-adoption analysis relating answer-adoption latency to the
correctness of the tool's output ($\Delta \times$ correctness). This was not
implemented, because assistant messages were not scored against the item key
during the trial. The behavioural risk surface it targeted --- the share of
answers submitted too quickly for independent verification --- is instead
reported through the direct-adoption rates of the answer-adoption-latency check
(Section~\ref{sec:res-checks}).

\subsection{Reproducibility and code of record}
\label{app:dev-repro}

The confirmatory results were produced by a single executed notebook
(\texttt{rct\_\allowbreak data\_\allowbreak literacy\_\allowbreak analysis\_\allowbreak 4\_\allowbreak FINAL\_\allowbreak 2026-07-21.\allowbreak ipynb}) importing
the shared estimator module \texttt{sap\_\allowbreak estimators.py}, run in a pinned
environment with a fixed random seed. This executed pipeline --- not any earlier
template or dummy-data copy --- is the code of record and is the version to be
archived with the registration once the embargo lifts. Two known, non-substantive
issues in the executed run are noted for transparency and do not affect any
reported estimate or verdict: the CONSORT completion-by-arm table is broken by a
participant-identifier key mismatch, so the completion counts in
the Results chapter (Section~\ref{sec:results}) were recomputed by hand; and one monitoring cell prints
a stale hard-coded narrative string (a leftover from the dummy-data pipeline)
around otherwise live numbers. Both are flagged for clean-up before the archived
code is finalised.
